## Verification of the customers, subscriptions and E2E audit reports

The core money claims hold up. The worst confirmed problems are:
- **Preview vs real invoice:** the subscription preview shows $120 for globex-seats, but the engine will bill 19/31 of that (about $73.55). A real test-clock invoice was billed "prorated 16/31" ($20.13) while its preview showed the full fee.
- **Canceled subscriptions say "Renews"**, on the detail page and in the list.
- **Voided and uncollectible invoices** keep their full amount due and a "pending" payment status in the API.
- **Dunning "Recovered"** counts voided and written-off runs.
- **EUR customers** see $ in Credit Balance, Billed usage and Margin.
- **Margin reads 100%** when no provider costs are configured.

SUB-12 was the one claim marked "inferred", and the code confirms it. None of the broken or confusing findings is fully refuted. Eleven are only partly right, and one is dropped (listed at the end).
